\chartersection{01}{AREA, PROBLEM AND SCOPE}
\textbf{Area:} Circular economy and environmental compliance; India's e-waste Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) reconciliation. Producer obligations and supporting records can disagree because of duplicates, quantity/category mismatches, incorrect periods, missing evidence or outdated rule references. Manual reconciliation must distinguish record errors from an unresolved shortfall.

\textbf{Scope:} One producer, one reporting period, limited equipment categories, structured records and a curated regulatory corpus. Use synthetic or de-identified data. Exclude live portal integration, regulatory filing and automatic legal compliance determination.

\par\vspace{2pt}
An auditable report will link findings to record IDs, calculations, applicable provisions/versions, open questions and next steps. Internal statuses: \emph{Reconciled; Discrepancy detected; Pending verification; Requires expert review}. These are proposed system statuses, not official regulatory classifications.

\chartersection{03}{AGENTIC CONCEPT MAPPING}
{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\bfseries}L{32mm}Y@{}}
Planning/reasoning & Plan checks $\rightarrow$ use tools $\rightarrow$ inspect results $\rightarrow$ investigate gaps $\rightarrow$ revise or stop within configured step limits.\\[2pt]
Tool use & Code checks duplicates, categories, quantities and periods; calculates the apparent gap from configured obligations and verified eligible credits.\\[2pt]
Agentic RAG & Select period-relevant provisions, assess evidence, re-query weak results and escalate unresolved applicability; retain source/version references.\\[2pt]
Observability & Trace actions, tool inputs/outputs, retrieved evidence, adaptations, stop reasons and final findings for every run.\\[2pt]
Evaluation & Golden cases; measure discrepancy precision/recall, arithmetic accuracy, citation support and appropriate escalation.\\[2pt]
Bonus concepts & Collector/auditor collaboration, human review, input/output validation and bounded loops.\\
\end{tabularx}}
\textbf{Agentic fit:} The agent chooses follow-up checks and retrieval in response to missing or conflicting evidence, rather than stopping at a fixed calculation or single prompt.

\chartersection{04}{DATA, TOOLS AND FEASIBILITY}
\textbf{Data:} Obligation tables; certificate/transaction CSVs with IDs, quantities/units, categories, periods and allocation links; official E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022, applicable amendments and CPCB guidance, tagged by version and effective period. Confirm document applicability during design.

\textbf{Proposed tools:} Python/pandas, SQLite, LangGraph, a searchable document index, a bounded LLM interface and structured traces. Regulatory sources: \href{https://cpcb.nic.in/}{CPCB}, \href{https://eprewaste.cpcb.gov.in/}{EPR portal} and \href{https://moef.gov.in/}{MoEFCC}.
\textbf{Experiment:} Compare fixed retrieval with agent-controlled re-querying on identical cases; measure evidence quality, detection and tool/call cost. Results remain to be measured.
