## Implemented first slice

`src/recycleguard/snapshots.py` supplies an internal SnapshotStore, using the shared
V02 connection/coordinator rather than another write path. New strict request
models SnapshotPublication and SnapshotReview are published as two additional
schemas; the original CP0 models are unchanged.

- Draft publication pins membership/coverage blobs, source receipts and the exact
  expected scope head. The service controls created_at and created_by.
- The head compare-and-swap, immutable snapshot insert, operation result and audit
  event share a single BEGIN IMMEDIATE transaction. A stale head cannot be overwritten.
- Trusted review binds target ID, membership digest and expected head, and creates
  a separate APPROVED or REJECTED child. The original draft bytes remain unchanged.
  Snapshot review is internal evidence review, not a statutory compliance verdict.
- Opaque reviewer capability/session checks run before request archival. A forged
  actor label or service capability cannot authorize a human review.
- Exact operation replay returns the archived snapshot without new domain writes.
  Known stale/digest rejections are archived; an unavailable audit sink leaves
  no falsely committed snapshot/head/result.
- Missing receipts remain part of draft membership; they do not become proof of
  complete coverage. Heads and snapshots survive reopening the store.
